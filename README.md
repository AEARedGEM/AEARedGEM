## 🌍 Building for Emerging Markets

A significant part of my work is influenced by the realities of emerging economies. Across Africa, businesses face challenges involving access to capital, expensive financial infrastructure, fragmented markets, and asset illiquidity.

I believe technology can help address these constraints when it is combined with sound economics, appropriate regulation, strong governance, and practical implementation. My long-term interest is therefore not simply building Web3 products—it is building **financial and technological infrastructure that can contribute to productive economic transformation.**

## 🧠 How I Think

I believe:

> **Finance should not merely circulate money. It should make productivity possible.**

I believe tokenization should answer a question more important than *"Can this asset be tokenized?"* The better question is:
> **"What economic problem does tokenization solve?"**

And beyond that:
> **"How do we build the infrastructure that allows the solution to scale?"**
